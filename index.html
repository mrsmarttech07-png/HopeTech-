What we do

Four services, planned to work together

Each stands on its own, or comes bundled as a single office fit-out.

01 · Surveillance

CCTV & Surveillance

Camera placement planned around your actual entry points and blind spots — not just wherever a wall socket happens to be.

  • Entry, exit & blind-spot coverage mapped during survey
  • Remote viewing set up on your phone before we leave
  • Recording storage sized to how long you need footage kept
02 · Infrastructure

Networking & Cabling

Structured cabling, switches, and racks laid out to handle your current headcount and the next round of growth.

  • Labelled, tested cable runs — not loose wires under a desk
  • Rack and switch capacity planned for future desks
  • Network diagram handed over at the end
03 · Connectivity

Office WiFi

Access points sited for even coverage across every desk and meeting room, with guest and staff networks kept separate.

  • Coverage tested in every room before handover
  • Separate guest network so visitors never touch your files
  • Bandwidth rules set per team or device type
04 · Workspace

Office IT Setup

Workstations, printers, and shared drives configured and labelled, so a new hire's desk works on day one.

  • New desks set up and tested before the new hire's first day
  • Shared drives and printers mapped consistently across machines
  • Login credentials documented and handed to your admin
